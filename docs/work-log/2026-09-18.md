  tokens, wall-clock). `agentdojo`-free like its siblings.
- **Benchmark** (`model_benchmark.py`): three persistent banking
  scenarios (all reading an injection vector; `user_task_5` rejected
  from the slice because its utility check is satisfiable by
  pre-existing state), no-defense vs `policies/agentdojo.yaml`,
  `SimulatedAgentDriver` reactions `retry`/`skip`/`abort`, fixtures
  committed under `examples/model_loop/fixtures/` (record → replay
  round-trip verified byte-for-byte on stats). Headline, pinned:

  | arm | reaction | utility | ASR | refusals | retries | tokens |
  |-----|----------|--------:|----:|---------:|--------:|-------:|
  | no-defense | — | 100% | 100% | 0 | 0 | ~10,213 |
  | apg-input-taint | retry | 20% | 0% | 21 | 15 | ~26,959 |
  | apg-input-taint | skip | 20% | 0% | 7 | 1 | ~10,196 |
  | apg-input-taint | abort | 0% | 0% | 5 | 0 | ~8,857 |

  Findings: ASR closes under every reaction; utility cost concentrates
  on sink turns; retry is pure spend; **abort loses even the query turn
  the policy never blocked** (the apology replaces the answer) — the
  over-compliance failure mode is now measurable.
- **Caveat.** No LLM API key exists in this environment (probe → 401),
