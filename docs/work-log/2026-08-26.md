## What shipped

Everything planned, in one run. Decisions worth recording (all in
`docs/design.md` "AgentDojo benchmark: no defense vs APG (R50)"):

1. **ASR denominator is armed episodes.** A pre-implementation probe of
   the real ground truths found 9 injection tasks with *empty* scripted
   ground truths (output-channel exfiltration) and slack's
   `injection_task_3` attacking via `get_webpage` (a reader). Excluding
   structurally unmeasurable pairs keeps the headline ASR honest instead
   of deflating both arms equally; the any-call rate is reported
   alongside and its defended residue (slack 60%) is the reader-borne
   exfiltration channel R53 is for.
2. **Fresh environment per episode** — episodes mutate suite state, so
   reuse would leak money movements/messages across pairs — and
   deterministic `<suite>:<user>x<injection>:<arm>` ids so the two arms'
   JSONL files line up row-for-row.
3. **The banking numbers are pinned by integration tests** against the
   published `docs/benchmarks/agentdojo.md` rows, so a policy or
   classification change that moves the results fails the suite.
